Q1: A company receives $12,000 cash for services to be performed next month. What is the correct entry?
  • A) Dr Cash $12,000; Cr Revenue $12,000
  • B) Dr Cash $12,000; Cr Unearned Revenue $12,000
  • C) Dr Revenue $12,000; Cr Cash $12,000
  • D) Dr Prepaid Revenue $12,000; Cr Cash $12,000
Show Answer
Answer: B
Cash received before earning = liability (Unearned Revenue). Revenue recognition principle — recognise when earned.
Q2: Which qualitative characteristic requires information to be complete, neutral, and free from error?
  • A) Relevance
  • B) Comparability
  • C) Faithful Representation
  • D) Timeliness
Show Answer
Answer: C
Faithful representation = complete, neutral, and free from error. Must reflect economic substance, not merely legal form.
Q3: On 1 June, XYZ pays $24,000 for a 12-month insurance policy. Adjusting entry on 30 June?
  • A) Dr Insurance Expense $24,000; Cr Prepaid $24,000
  • B) Dr Insurance Expense $2,000; Cr Prepaid $2,000
  • C) Dr Prepaid $2,000; Cr Insurance Expense $2,000
  • D) No entry needed
Show Answer
Answer: B
$24,000 ÷ 12 = $2,000 per month. One month expired.

ETW1001 — Business Statistics

Hypothesis Testing Framework6 Steps
Step 1State H₀ and H₁
Step 2Choose α (usually 0.05)
Step 3Calculate test statistic
Step 4Determine critical value / p-value
Step 5Decision: reject or fail to reject H₀
Step 6Conclusion in context
⚠ Exam Trap
We "reject" or "fail to reject" H₀ — never "accept" H₀. p-value < α means reject H₀.
Key FormulasMemorize

Confidence Interval (Proportion)
p̂ ± z(α/2) × √[p̂(1−p̂)/n]

b = Σ(x−x̄)(y−ȳ) ÷ Σ(x−x̄)²
