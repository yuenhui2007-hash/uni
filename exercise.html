💡 Exam Strategy & Tips

⏰ Time Management

Allocate time proportionally to marks. Don't spend 20 minutes on a 5-mark question.

📝 Read Carefully

Watch for "except", "not", "always", "never" in MCQs. Underline key terms.

✅ Show Working

Even if your final answer is wrong, you may get partial marks for correct method.

🔄 Check Balance

Financial statements must balance. If they don't, recheck your calculations.

📚 Definitions Matter

Memorise key definitions verbatim. Short-answer questions test precise language.

🎯 Start Strong

Begin with questions you know best to build confidence and secure marks early.
