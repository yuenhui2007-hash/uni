▶ Remedies for Breach

RemedyWhen AwardedKey Point
DamagesStandard remedy for all breachesPut injured party in position as if contract performed
Liquidated DamagesGenuine pre-estimate in contractNot a penalty — must reflect actual loss estimate
Specific PerformanceUnique subject matter (land, art)Discretionary; not for personal services
InjunctionRestraining party from breachNegative specific performance

▶ SWOT Analysis

✅ Strengths

Internal positive attributes the organization possesses. Strong brand, skilled workforce, proprietary technology, good cash flow.

⚠️ Weaknesses

Internal negative attributes. High debt, outdated systems, skill gaps, poor location.

🔵 Opportunities

External factors to exploit. Market growth, new technology, competitor weakness, regulatory changes.

🔴 Threats

External factors that could harm. New competitors, economic downturn, changing consumer tastes, supply chain risks.
