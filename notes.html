▶ SWOT Analysis

Strengths

Internal positive attributes the organization possesses. Strong brand, skilled workforce, proprietary technology, good cash flow.

Weaknesses

Internal negative attributes. High debt, outdated systems, skill gaps, poor location.

Opportunities

External factors to exploit. Market growth, new technology, competitor weakness, regulatory changes.

Threats

External factors that could harm. New competitors, economic downturn, changing consumer tastes, supply chain risks.
